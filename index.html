Twelve stone villas, half buried in the dunes

A small retreat on the edge of the Empty Quarter, where the evening is the main event.

Built from the ground it stands on

The walls are rammed from the site's own sand and lime, sixty centimetres thick, so rooms stay cool at noon and warm at three in the morning. Every villa faces west across open dunes, and there is no other building on that horizon.

There are no televisions and the lights are dimmed after ten, so the stars come down to meet you.

A night here runs to the sky's schedule

From sundowner to breakfast, this is one evening in mid-January, drawn to scale. The times move with the seasons; the order never does.

Dinner is never in the same place twice

Chef Noor Haddad cooks over date-palm wood for twenty-six people at most. Some nights the table is in the courtyard, some nights it is a kilometre out on the sand, lit by two hundred candles and the tail-lights of the 4×4 that carried it there.

There is one menu each evening, written that afternoon from what came in from Liwa's farms. Tell us what you cannot eat when you book and it is handled without fuss.

Half board is included in Courtyard Villas and the Ridge House.

Before you come

When can I check in and out?

Villas are ready from 2 pm and we ask you to leave by noon. Transfers are timed to reach the dunes before sunset, so most guests arrive around five.

Can children stay?

Children aged six and over are welcome in Courtyard Villas and the Ridge House. Dune Rooms are for two adults.

What does the rate include?

Breakfast, sundowners on the ridge and telescope hour. Courtyard Villas and the Ridge House also include dinner. Transfers are charged separately, one 4×4 each way for up to three guests.

Can I cancel?

Cancel up to 21 days before arrival and nothing is charged. After that, the first night is charged.

When is Halavar open?

From October to April. We close from May to September, when the afternoon heat passes 45 °C.

Is there phone signal?

Signal reaches the main house only. Villas have Wi-Fi on request, though most guests leave it off.
